Educator first. Builder always.

I am Edwin Jaquez, founder of PalmChat Innovations. I help people make sense of new technology, then put it to work.

Start a conversation

I have taught computer science, led a school, coached teachers across New York City, and built software. PalmChat brings all of it into one place.

My work sits where education and technology meet: professional learning, AI fluency, curriculum, and the products and workflows that help people actually adopt something new.

The question I keep coming back to: how can people use this technology well, responsibly, and for long enough that it matters?

Why the bird? The cigua palmera is the Dominican Republic's national bird. Palmchats build their nests together in the crowns of royal palms. That is how I like to work.
