About Edwin

Educator first. Designer always.

Edwin Jaquez founded PalmChat Innovations to help education teams turn promising ideas into learning people can understand, use, and sustain.

EJFounder · Educator · Designer

Temporary founder portrait placeholder

Edwin is an educator, computer science teacher, school culture and instructional leader, professional learning consultant, curriculum and program designer, AI and emerging technology facilitator, and founder building education products.

His perspective is shaped by the work of teaching, leading, coaching, facilitating, and designing alongside the people responsible for bringing ideas into practice.

PalmChat creates room for rigorous thinking and approachable learning. The goal is not technology for its own sake, but clearer decisions, stronger experiences, and programs that make sense in the environments where they will live.

“Make learning joyful, rigorous, practical, and inclusive.”
