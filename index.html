About Edwin

Founder-led learning design grounded in classroom experience.

Edwin Jaquez is an educator, computer science teacher, school culture and instructional leader, professional learning consultant, curriculum and program designer, AI and emerging technology facilitator, and founder building education products.

His work brings together classroom realities, careful strategy, and approachable design—helping teams make learning rigorous, useful, joyful, and inclusive.

Read Edwin’s longer biography

Edwin’s work centers on helping adults and students connect learning goals, instructional practice, and meaningful change. He supports teams that want professional learning to feel relevant, leadership to feel actionable, and curriculum to feel coherent from idea to implementation.

He works in English and Spanish and approaches every engagement with curiosity, clarity, and respect for the expertise already present in a community.
