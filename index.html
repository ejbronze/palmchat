About Edwin

Founder-led learning design grounded in classroom experience.

Edwin Jaquez is an educator, computer science teacher, school culture and instructional leader, professional learning consultant, curriculum and program designer, and founder building education products.

He brings a practical lens shaped by teaching, leadership, and learning design. PalmChat Innovations partners with schools and education organizations to build programs, experiences, and professional learning that are rigorous, usable, and grounded in real classroom realities.

Read more

Edwin’s work centers on helping adults and students connect learning goals, instructional practice, and meaningful change. He supports teams that want professional learning to feel relevant, leadership to feel actionable, and curriculum to feel coherent from idea to implementation.

He is bilingual in English and Spanish and supports learning experiences rooted in joy, rigor, practicality, and inclusion. PalmChat Innovations is designed to make learning more approachable, engaging, and relevant—while still being clear, strategic, and accountable to real outcomes.

Languages: English and Spanish

Approach: Making learning joyful, rigorous, practical, and inclusive.
