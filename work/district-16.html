Professional Learning · Leadership

NYC District 16 Leadership Professional Learning

Moving from district priorities to school-level action, ownership, monitoring, and student growth.

PalmChat role

Professional learning designer and facilitator supporting leadership teams.

Context

School leaders were working to translate shared district priorities into coherent action across individual school communities.

01

Challenge

The work required a practical bridge between broad priorities and the routines, decisions, ownership, and evidence leaders could use at the school level.

02

Approach

Facilitated collaborative learning and planning that connected strategic priorities to school-level ownership, implementation routines, and evidence of progress.

03

Deliverables

  • Leadership workshops
  • Planning conversations
  • Implementation frameworks
  • Monitoring tools
04

Tools + methods

  • Facilitation
  • Strategic planning
  • Instructional leadership
  • Stakeholder engagement
05

Result / intended impact

Designed to support clearer ownership, visible next steps, and stronger alignment between leadership action and student growth.
