AI Fluency Professional Learning

Building AI literacy through prompting, ethical use, bias, guardrails, and classroom application.

role

AI literacy learning designer and professional learning facilitator.

context

Educators were exploring generative AI while navigating fast-moving tools, uneven familiarity, and important questions about classroom use.

category

AI integration / professional learning

Challenge

Participants needed a practical learning experience that moved beyond hype while making space for experimentation, ethics, bias, and responsible guardrails.

Approach

Skills came before tools. Sessions combined hands-on prompting, evaluating output, and reflection on classroom relevance with attention to equity, bias, ethical use, and learning goals.

Deliverables

  • AI fluency sessions
  • Prompting frameworks
  • Discussion guides
  • Ethical-use resources

Tools and methods

  • Prompt development
  • Hands-on exploration
  • Ethics protocols
  • Educator coaching

Intended impact

Designed to help educators evaluate, use, and discuss AI with greater clarity and purpose.
