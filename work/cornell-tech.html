K–12 Computer Science Coaching

Supporting K–8 educators with CS integration, computational thinking, and classroom practice.

PalmChat role

Computer science coach and professional learning facilitator.

Context

K–8 educators were working to bring computer science and computational thinking into varied classroom contexts.

01

Challenge

Teachers needed practical support translating broad computer science goals into instructionally sound experiences they could adapt and sustain.

02

Approach

Combined coaching, classroom support, collaborative planning, and professional learning to connect computational thinking with day-to-day instruction.

03

Deliverables

  • Coaching cycles
  • Professional development
  • Planning support
  • Integration resources
04

Tools + methods

  • Computer science pedagogy
  • Computational thinking
  • Teacher coaching
  • Curriculum integration
05

Intended impact

Designed to strengthen teacher confidence and support sustainable computer science practice.
