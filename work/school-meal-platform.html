School Meal Operations Platform

PalmChat designed and developed an education platform that brings together school-based operations, user workflows, administrative tools, and reporting in one streamlined experience.

PalmChat role

Education product strategist, user-experience designer, workflow designer, and full-stack product developer.

Context

This engagement brought together education product strategy, school operations technology, interface development, and role-based platform planning.

01

Challenge

The work required a coherent product experience for school-based operations without exposing private workflows, business rules, student information, or proprietary requirements.

02

Approach

Developed the platform at a capability level around user experience, administrative tools, reporting concepts, and operational problem-solving. Public details remain intentionally limited.

03

Deliverables

  • User experience design

  • Role-based platform planning
04

Tools + methods

Designed to support a more streamlined operational experience while protecting confidential product and school information.
